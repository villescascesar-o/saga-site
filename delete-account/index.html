Delete your Saga account and data

This page explains how to delete your account and your data in Saga (also called Saga: AI Journal), the journaling app by Cesar Villescas. You can do it yourself in the app, or ask us by email.

Option 1: delete your account in the app (fastest)

  1. Open Saga and log in.
  2. Tap the person icon at the top right to open Settings.
  3. Scroll to Account and tap Delete account.
  4. Confirm. On iPhone you are asked for your password; on Android you may be asked to log in again first, so the deletion is secure.

Deletion starts immediately and cannot be undone.

Option 2: ask us to delete your account

If you cannot open the app, email villescascesar@gmail.com from the email address on your account with the subject "Delete my Saga account". We will delete the account and reply when it is done, within 30 days.

What is deleted

  • Your journal entries, photos, videos and voice notes
  • Your beneficiaries
  • Your profile, settings and streak
  • Everything the AI companion derived from your journal: chats, memory and daily notes
  • Your subscription record and your user record at our subscription provider (RevenueCat)

What is kept, and for how long

  • Copies in backups or logs can remain for a limited time before they are overwritten.
  • Analytics events already sent are pseudonymous (no names, emails or journal text). Email us and we will delete them too.
  • We may keep limited records where the law requires it.
  • Your subscription is not cancelled by deleting your account. Cancel it in your store settings (Apple ID subscriptions, or Google Play: Payments and subscriptions) so you are not charged again.

Delete some data without deleting your account

  • Entries, photos, videos, voice notes: open the entry and delete them, or delete the text. What the AI derived from them is removed too.
  • Chats with the companion: long-press a chat in the Chats list and delete it, or use the "..." menu inside the chat.
  • Anything else (for example turning off the AI features and deleting what was derived): email villescascesar@gmail.com and tell us what to remove.
